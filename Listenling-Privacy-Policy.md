# Listenling Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 23, 2026**

Listenling is a macOS music companion developed by Xian Zhang. This policy explains what information Listenling processes and when information is sent to other services.

## Information Processed on Your Mac

Listenling reads song information from Spotify and Apple Music, such as the song title, artist, album, playback position, and artwork, to display the player and provide playback controls. macOS may ask you to authorize this access.

If you enable the audio-reactive equalizer and grant macOS permission, Listenling analyzes audio from the playing app on your Mac to animate the equalizer. Audio is analyzed locally. Listenling does not record, save, or send the audio.

Listenling stores settings and cached lyrics locally on your Mac. Cached lyrics remain until the cache or app data is removed.

## Lyrics Lookup

When Listenling looks up lyrics, it sends the song’s title, artist, album, and duration to [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net). LRCLIB returns matching lyrics. The request is made directly from your Mac to LRCLIB; Listenling’s developer does not receive the lookup request. Please refer to LRCLIB for its own privacy practices.

## Album Artwork

When Spotify provides an artwork URL, Listenling downloads that image from the URL provided by Spotify so it can display the artwork. Apple Music artwork is obtained through the Apple Music integration. Listenling does not send audio recordings or playback controls to these artwork sources.

## Information Listenling Does Not Collect

Listenling does not require an account and does not operate its own advertising or analytics service. The developer does not receive your song information, audio, or lyrics searches through Listenling.

## Your Choices

You can turn off lyrics and the audio-reactive equalizer in Listenling’s settings. You can also manage Listenling’s permissions in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.

## Children’s Privacy

Listenling is not designed to collect personal information from children.

This policy may be updated if Listenling’s features or data practices change. The latest version will be available at this page.
