## Features

- **Notch player:** See album artwork and playback at a glance, then open the full player when you want more controls.
- **Menu-bar player:** Open a playback popover from the menu bar.
- **Mini player:** Keep a compact, movable player on screen.
- **Playback controls:** Play, pause, skip, seek, shuffle, and repeat where supported.
- **Synced lyrics:** Look up lyrics through [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net) when available.
- **Audio-reactive equalizer:** Animate the bars from music audio, with macOS permission. Audio is analyzed on your Mac and is not recorded or sent by Listenling.

## Compatibility

- macOS 13 or later
- Spotify or Apple Music
- Normal audio applications, including broswers
- Audio-reactive equalizer requires macOS 14.2 or later; earlier versions use a simulated animation.

## Privacy

For lyrics lookup, Listenling sends the song’s title, artist, album, and duration to LRCLIB. Audio analysis happens locally on your Mac.
